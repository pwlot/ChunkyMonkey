# Privacy

Last updated: 28 August 2026.

The data controller for the ChunkyMonkey site and Pro purchase records is Pawel Pachniewski. Contact: [chunkymonkey@pwlot.com](mailto:chunkymonkey@pwlot.com).

ChunkyMonkey is local-first. It has no repository service and collects no telemetry. Git remotes and optional tools run only when you invoke them using the accounts and endpoints you configure. Bug Report opens a prefilled email for your review. Common secrets, absolute local paths, and recognized remote URLs are redacted automatically. Diagnostics stay local unless you attach them yourself.

The site stores your theme preference in your browser. Site hosting may process standard request data to deliver and secure pages. During a Pro purchase, the checkout service processes payment and billing details. License fulfillment receives the checkout email, payment status, and transaction identifiers needed to generate and deliver your key. The email delivery service receives the destination email and license message. These services process data under their own privacy terms.

Purchase and license delivery use data to perform the purchase contract. Support and security processing rely on legitimate interests, and accounting records are retained for legal obligations. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and may complain to your data-protection authority. Send requests to [chunkymonkey@pwlot.com](mailto:chunkymonkey@pwlot.com). Records required by law may be retained.

Do not attach secrets, credentials, private source, proprietary assets, or private dataset contents to support or security email.
