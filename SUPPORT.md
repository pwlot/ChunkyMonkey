# Support

Use Bug Report inside ChunkyMonkey for product problems. It opens a prefilled email to [p.pachniewski@gmail.com](mailto:p.pachniewski@gmail.com) with the subject `ChunkyMonkey bug report: [your subject]`. Review it before sending.

If the app cannot start, send the same email manually. Do not attach secrets, credentials, or private project files.

Support development:

https://www.pwlot.com/#support
